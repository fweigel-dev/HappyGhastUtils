Happy Ghast Utils is a client-side Fabric mod that enhances the Happy Ghast experience in Minecraft.

## Features

### Hydration Indicator

See the hydration level of Dried Ghasts.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fweigel-dev/HappyGhastUtils/refs/heads/main/README/hydration_indicator.png" width="480" style="border-radius: 10px"/>

---

### Baby Timer

A countdown timer above every Ghastling showing the time remaining until it grows into a Happy Ghast.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fweigel-dev/HappyGhastUtils/refs/heads/main/README/baby_timer.png" width="600" style="border-radius: 10px"/>

---

### Navigation & Autopilot

Set a destination by entering X, Y, Z coordinates, the autopilot will navigate you to the target.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fweigel-dev/HappyGhastUtils/refs/heads/main/README/navigation.gif" width="600" style="border-radius: 10px"/>

---

### Sound Volume Control

Adjust the volume of Happy Ghast and Ghastling sounds independently of the main game volume.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fweigel-dev/HappyGhastUtils/refs/heads/main/README/settings.png" width="600" style="border-radius: 10px"/>
